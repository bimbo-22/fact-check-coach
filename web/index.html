    Why you can trust it, and where you shouldn't

    🧠 The AI never decides

    It only reads: it pulls out the claim and says whether each article agrees. The verdict comes from fixed rules anyone can audit.

    🛡️ Every quote is checked

    If the AI quotes an article, code checks the quote is really there. Made-up quotes are thrown out and counted, and you can see the count.

    ❓ “Unsure” is a real answer

    “Unverified” and “still developing” are normal results, and confidence is always shown. Breaking news waits for at least two outlets.

    🔒 Nothing is kept

    No accounts, no stored posts, screenshots, usernames or chats. The cache keeps only a hash of the claim.

    💬 Opinions aren't “fact-checked”

    In posts and in comments, opinions are labelled as opinions, not judged as true or false.

    ⚡ Small model, on purpose

    We never ask the model to know facts, so a small open model (IBM Granite) is enough. That uses less energy and costs less.

    Honest limits

    • Our outlets are mostly English-language (plus French) and Western, so many local or non-English claims come back “unverified”.
    • We read the text in screenshots. A photo or video without text (like an AI-made image) isn't analysed yet.
    • Instagram doesn't let apps read posts from a link, so a screenshot or the pasted caption is needed.
    • Our outlets lean centre to centre-left, and we say so.
